## New campaign Lights and Shadows has been released!

Meet _Kenba_, heroine of the human faction, and learn more about the origins of the Aberrant Cult, 
lead the alliance of Order and contain the hordes of Chaos.

New faction to master, with a wide variety of units to face new and old rivals.
Challenging confrontations and new strategies to apply with a nostalgic twist from previous campaigns.
Join forces with all the troops you have unlocked so far and take down the Aberrant Cult and their malevolent leaders!
