## New campaign Fire and Shadows has been released!

The new campaign is made up of 14 Mind of War-style encounters, where you can learn of the _Aberrant Cult's assault_ on the eastern forests, 
the rise of nature and fantastic wildlife, and the horrors that lie beneath the mountains; and stop the corruption and destruction wrought by the 
heretics from destroying the world, fighting with _Galendril_ and his unstoppable army.

Also for this update we have worked on implementing compatibility with MAC devices,
so those of you who have been waiting since launch to play on your favourite platform will now be able to play.
_(We ask for a little patience if any problems arise as we will continue to work on making everything look and work with the highest quality possible)._
